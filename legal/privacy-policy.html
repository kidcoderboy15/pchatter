🏓 Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 18, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to Pickle Chatter ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Please read this privacy policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this privacy policy, please do not access the application.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide Directly

  • Account Information: Email address, username, display name, phone number (optional)
  • Profile Information: Profile picture, bio, skill level, location (city/state)
  • User-Generated Content: Game results, scores, statistics, ratings of other players, comments, availability schedules
  • Communication Data: Messages and interactions with other users within the app

Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version, unique device identifiers
  • Usage Data: App features used, time spent in app, navigation patterns, crash reports
  • Location Data: Approximate location (city/state level) to help you find local games and courts
  • Performance Data: App performance metrics, errors, and diagnostics

Information from Third Parties

  • Authentication Providers: If you sign in with third-party services (Google, Apple), we receive basic profile information they provide
  • Payment Processors: If we implement in-app purchases, payment information is processed by third parties (Apple, Google) - we do not store credit card details

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for:

  • Providing Services: Creating and managing your account, facilitating game scheduling, tracking statistics
  • Social Features: Connecting you with other players, enabling group creation, sharing game results
  • Personalization: Customizing your experience, showing relevant games and players nearby
  • Communication: Sending notifications about games, friend requests, match confirmations
  • Analytics: Understanding how users interact with the app, improving features
  • Security: Detecting and preventing fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access
  • Legal Compliance: Complying with legal obligations and enforcing our terms

Location Data

We collect approximate location data to:

  • Show you nearby pickleball courts and games
  • Connect you with local players and groups
  • Display location-relevant content

You can control location permissions through your device settings. Disabling location will limit some features but the core app functionality will remain available.

Camera and Photo Access

We request camera access for:

  • Scanning QR codes to quickly join games or groups
  • Taking profile pictures (optional)

We request photo library access for:

  • Selecting existing photos for your profile (optional)

You can deny these permissions and still use most app features. Photos are stored securely and never shared without your explicit consent.

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do NOT sell your personal data. We may share your information in these limited circumstances:

With Other Users

  • Public Profile: Your username, profile picture, skill level, and statistics are visible to other users in your groups
  • Game Results: Scores and statistics from games you participate in are shared with other participants
  • Ratings: Ratings you give and receive are visible to relevant users

With Service Providers

  • Supabase: Backend infrastructure and database hosting
  • Expo/EAS: App build and delivery infrastructure
  • Sentry (if enabled): Crash reporting and error monitoring
  • Analytics Services: Anonymized usage analytics

All service providers are bound by data protection agreements and only access data necessary for their services.

For Legal Reasons

We may disclose your information if required by law, court order, or governmental authority, or to:

  • Enforce our Terms of Service
  • Protect our rights, property, or safety
  • Prevent fraud or illegal activity
  • Protect the safety of users or the public

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data:

  • Encryption: Data in transit is encrypted using TLS/SSL
  • Access Controls: Row-Level Security (RLS) policies ensure users can only access their authorized data
  • Authentication: Secure authentication via Supabase Auth
  • Regular Audits: Periodic security reviews and vulnerability assessments
  • Limited Access: Only authorized personnel have access to user data

However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Privacy Rights

All Users

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction: Update or correct inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and personal data
  • Opt-Out: Unsubscribe from promotional communications

GDPR Rights (EU/EEA Users)

  • Portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Restriction: Request restriction of processing in certain circumstances
  • Objection: Object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent for data processing at any time

CCPA Rights (California Users)

  • Know: Know what personal information is collected, used, and shared
  • Delete: Request deletion of personal information
  • Opt-Out: Opt-out of the sale of personal information (we do not sell data)
  • Non-Discrimination: Not receive discriminatory treatment for exercising privacy rights

Children's Privacy

Pickle Chatter is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us, and we will delete such information promptly.

Your continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
