FAQ

Questions? Answers.

OpenClaw is a free, open-source autonomous AI agent platform. Unlike simple chatbots, OpenClaw agents can execute complex multi-step tasks, make autonomous decisions, and operate across multiple channels (email, SMS, voice, chat) simultaneously. Think of it as hiring a digital employee that works 24/7 without breaks.

ChatGPT is a conversation tool — you ask it questions, it answers. OpenClaw is an autonomous agent — it proactively takes action. It can monitor your email, answer phone calls, update your CRM, schedule meetings, follow up with leads, and make decisions on its own. It doesn't wait to be prompted — it works autonomously around the clock.

Most agents are fully operational within 1–3 days. We've refined our process to move fast without cutting corners. Discovery on day one, architecture and prompt engineering on day two, deployment and testing on day three. Some simpler deployments go live in a single day.

Not at all. We handle all the technical setup, configuration, and deployment. You interact with your agent through natural conversation — just like talking to a team member. We also provide hands-on training so you know exactly how to monitor performance and request changes.

Your agent runs on dedicated infrastructure — either a Mac Mini at your location or a cloud VPS. This means your data stays on your hardware, your agent is always available, and you're not sharing resources with anyone else. We help you choose the best option for your needs.

After the one-time deployment fee, your only costs are the AI model API usage (typically $50–200/month depending on volume) and optional ongoing optimization retainers. There are no licensing fees since OpenClaw is open source. We're transparent about every cost upfront.
